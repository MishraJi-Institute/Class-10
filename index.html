AI Project Cycle — Core Concept

First, understand the words themselves.

AI Project Cycle

AI problem ko solve karne ke liye follow kiya jane wala step-by-step process.

Project

Kisi specific goal ko achieve karne ke liye planned work.

Cycle

Steps ek baar karke khatam nahi hote; improvement ke liye process repeat ho sakta hai.

"Cycle" means feedback milne par wapas pehle stage par jana bhi possible hai.

The Six Stages of AI Project Cycle

1

Problem Scoping

Samasya ko clearly define karna.

2

Data Acquisition

Zaroori data collect karna.

3

Data Exploration

Data ko samajhna aur patterns dekhna.

4

Modelling

Data se model banana / pattern seekhana.

5

Evaluation

Model ke result ko test karna.

6

Deployment

Real use mein lagana aur feedback se sudharna.

Stage 1: Problem Scoping

Problem ko AI solution mein badalne se pehle uska scope (boundaries, affected people, goal) clear karna zaroori hai.

WHO

Kaun prabhavit hai?

WHAT

Asal samasya kya hai?

WHERE

Samasya kahan hai?

WHY

Goal kya hai?

School Plant Watering System

  • WHO: School gardeners and students.
  • WHAT & WHERE: Plants are watered too early/late in the school garden.
  • WHY: To use available data to predict when a plant needs watering.
Final Goal: "Can we predict when a school plant needs watering using available data?"

Data Acquisition

Required data ko collect karna. Data relevant (problem se juda hua) aur reliable (bharosemand) hona chahiye.

Plant Example:

Collecting soil-moisture readings and temperature from school garden sensors.

Data Exploration

Raw data ko inspect aur visualize karke hidden patterns dhoondhna.

Pattern: Baar-baar dikhne wala trend.
Outlier: Sabse alag value.

Plant Example:

Graphing the data to see patterns between temperature and when soil gets dry.

Modelling & Evaluation

Real-World Example: Spam Email Filter

4

Modelling (Pattern Seekhana)

Give the model examples of "spam" and "not spam" emails (Training Data). Model unse words ke patterns seekhta hai aur naye email par Prediction deta hai.

5

Evaluation (Test Karna)

Model ke result ko test data par check karna. Dekhna ki kitne predictions Accurate hain, kitne Errors hue, aur kya result Fair hai.

*Evaluation ke baad, agar errors zyada hain, toh cycle wapas improvement ke liye peeche jata hai.

AI Domains

AI kis type ke data par kaam kar raha hai?

DATA

Numbers / Structured Data se patterns aur predictions nikalna.

Ex: Shop checking past sales records to predict next month's demand.

VISION

Images / Video se objects aur visual features ko identify karna.

Ex: Face unlock in smartphones or Traffic cameras detecting vehicles.

NLP

Human Language (Text/Speech) ko computer se process karwana.

Ex: Voice assistants (Alexa/Siri) understanding "Set an alarm".

Ethical Frameworks — Core Meaning

AI sirf technical system nahi; iske decisions logon par impact karte hain.

Framework

Kisi problem ko systematically handle karne ka structured tareeqa.

Ethics

Values jo humein right-wrong ya responsible choices par sochne mein madad karte hain.

Ethical Framework

Ethical decision lene ke liye structured guidance ka set.

Technical asks: "Can we build it?"
Ethics asks: "How should we build/use it responsibly?"

Real-World Context: Social Impacts of AI

AI Bias

Agar training data balanced nahi hai, toh AI unfair pattern seekh sakta hai.

Impact: Different groups ke liye results unequal ho sakte hain (Unfair outcomes).

Data Privacy

Location, photos, aur activity personal data hai.

Impact: Galat use ya access se privacy aur user trust break hota hai.

AI & Employment

AI repetitive tasks ko automate kar raha hai.

Impact: Purane jobs change honge aur naye skills/roles ki zaroorat padegi (Reskilling).

AI for Children

AI tools ka use age-appropriate aur safe hona chahiye.

Guidance: Never share personal info with AI. Use under trusted adult supervision.

Types of Ethical Frameworks

1. Sector-Based

Kisi specific field ke ethical concerns par focus karna.

Healthcare Bioethics

2. Value-Based

Fundamental ethical values ke aadhar par decision lena.

  • Rights-Based: Protect people's rights & freedom.
  • Utility-Based: Maximize benefit, minimize harm.
  • Virtue-Based: Focus on good character (honesty).

Bioethics & Its 4 Principles

Healthcare aur life sciences se jude ethical questions ka structured study = Bioethics

1. Autonomy

Vyakti ki informed choice aur decision ka samman.

2. Do Not Harm

Anavashyak (unnecessary) harm se bachna.

3. Max Benefit

Well-being badhana aur positive outcomes lana.

4. Justice

Benefits aur harms ko fairness ke saath baantna.

Case Study: Hospital AI

If an AI identifies high-risk patients, doctors must ensure the AI is fair across all groups (Justice), benefits patient care (Max Benefit), doesn't cause medical errors (Do Not Harm), and patients are informed (Autonomy).
